— About Techfest

Where Innovation Meets Imagination

Techfest, the annual Science & Technology Festival of IIT Bombay, is Asia's largest science and technology festival. Since its inception in 1998, it has been a platform for the brightest minds to compete, collaborate and create.

Spanning three action-packed days, Techfest hosts over 250 events across robotics, computer science, aerospace, biotech, and more — drawing participants from 135+ countries.
